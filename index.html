02 // ABOUT

Systems that leave the lab

I build AI systems that work in the real world, not just in notebooks. At Zenon Robotics I lead the full AI stack for Robin, a service robot working in front of real customers in the UAE. I handle the vision, the multilingual speech, the motion, and the ROS2 infrastructure that keeps it all alive. Before this I spent a year at the Colorado School of Mines turning raw methane sensor streams into an anomaly detection system that hits 99% accuracy and still runs in production. More recently I have been building RAG and multi-agent LLM systems that are measured, not just demoed: FieldForge (open source) and an invoice-review copilot.

I care about deployment, reliability, and interfaces people actually use. When I'm not shipping robots, I'm usually somewhere with a camera, on a long road with a good playlist, or having a chai and writing code just for the fun of it. That eye for framing follows me into everything I build.
